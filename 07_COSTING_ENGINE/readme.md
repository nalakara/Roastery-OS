# Costing Engine

## Purpose

Costing Engine defines the centralized costing philosophy, valuation continuity, and operational economics framework used across Roastery OS.

This module acts as:
- the operational valuation layer,
- profitability continuity system,
- and cost intelligence foundation
for all inventory transformation workflows.

Costing Engine standardizes:
- how operational value evolves,
- how inventory valuation propagates,
- and how production economics remain traceable across the ecosystem.

---

# Core Philosophy

Costing Engine treats costing as:
- operational value evolution,
not merely:
- accounting calculation,
- retail pricing,
- or bookkeeping infrastructure.

The system preserves:
- transformation-aware valuation,
- deterministic costing continuity,
- profitability visibility,
- and operational economic traceability.

Costing workflows should remain:
- deterministic,
- traceable,
- modular,
- and operationally understandable.

Human-Centered Philosophy

	•	production teams,
	•	and business owners.

	•	understand cost evolution,
	•	evaluate profitability,

Deterministic Costing Principle
Critical costing behavior must remain deterministic.
Examples:
	•	inventory valuation continuity,
	•	yield-adjusted costing,
	•	overhead allocation,
	•	and profitability relationships.
Costing systems should:
	•	produce predictable outcomes,
	•	preserve operational integrity,
	•	and remain auditable.
The system should avoid:
	•	hidden valuation mutation,
	•	ambiguous costing behavior,
	•	and disconnected economic continuity.

AI Boundary Philosophy
AI systems may:
	•	analyze profitability behavior,
	•	identify costing anomalies,
	•	recommend operational optimization,
	•	and support forecasting systems.
However: AI must not autonomously manipulate deterministic valuation relationships.
Critical costing continuity must remain:
	•	explicit,
	•	traceable,
	•	deterministic,
	•	and human-auditable.
