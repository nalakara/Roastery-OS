# Profitability Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the profitability philosophy and operational profitability behavior used inside the Costing Engine of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Profitability Principles is to:
- preserve operational profitability visibility,
- standardize margin awareness across workflows,
- maintain transformation-aware economic understanding,
- and define how operational behavior affects business sustainability.

Profitability represents:
- operational economic health,
not merely:
- accounting profit reporting.

Profitability is one of the core operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats profitability as:
- operational economic behavior,
- transformation-aware business sustainability,
- and workflow-driven margin visibility.

Profitability is not merely:
- accounting reporting,
- financial summary,
- or sales arithmetic.

Profitability represents:
- how operational reality affects business economics over time.

The system should preserve:
- profitability continuity,
- valuation visibility,
- and deterministic economic relationships.
