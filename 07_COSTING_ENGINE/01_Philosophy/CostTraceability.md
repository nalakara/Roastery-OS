# Cost Traceability

## Purpose

This document defines the cost traceability philosophy and operational economic lineage behavior used inside the Costing Engine of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Cost Traceability is to:
- preserve valuation lineage continuity,
- maintain operational economic transparency,
- support profitability visibility,
- and define how cost relationships remain traceable throughout inventory transformation workflows.

Cost traceability represents:
- operational economic lineage.

Cost traceability is one of the foundational intelligence layers inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats cost traceability as:
- operational economic continuity,
- valuation lineage preservation,
- and transformation-aware profitability visibility.

Cost traceability is not merely:
- accounting audit history,
- bookkeeping reference,
- or financial reporting infrastructure.

Cost traceability represents:
- how operational reality creates economic evolution over time.

The system should preserve:
- valuation continuity,
- profitability lineage,
- deterministic economic relationships,
- and operational readability.

	•	deterministic economic lineage.

	•	and why profitability changed.

	•	operational understanding, not merely:

	•	overhead propagation,

	•	produce predictable outcomes,

	•	and remain auditable.

	•	ambiguous economic behavior,
	•	disconnected valuation continuity,
	•	and hidden profitability mutation.
