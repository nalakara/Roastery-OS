# MVP Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the MVP boundaries and implementation scope limitations for the Costing Engine inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of MVP Boundaries is to:
- preserve architectural focus,
- maintain operational realism,
- avoid premature financial complexity,
- and establish a scalable operational economics foundation.

The MVP Costing Engine should prioritize:
- operational valuation continuity,
- profitability visibility,
- deterministic economic behavior,
- and human-readable operational economics.

The MVP is intended to establish:
- a strong operational costing foundation,
not:
- a full enterprise accounting system.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS evolves progressively.

The MVP Costing Engine should therefore focus on:
- practical operational economics,
- deterministic valuation continuity,
- transformation-aware costing,
- and operational profitability visibility.

The MVP should avoid:
- enterprise accounting bureaucracy,
- industrial finance infrastructure,
- and premature AI autonomy.

The goal is:
- operational usefulness first,
- ecosystem sophistication later.

Human-Centered MVP Principle
The MVP should remain:
	•	approachable,
	•	understandable,
	•	and operationally useful.
Operators should be able to:
	•	understand inventory economics,
	•	evaluate profitability,
	•	and follow valuation continuity without accounting-level complexity.
The MVP should avoid:
	•	ERP intimidation,
	•	financial bureaucracy,
	•	and excessive economic abstraction.

Simplicity Principle
The MVP should prioritize:
	•	economic clarity,
	•	profitability visibility,
	•	and deterministic valuation behavior.
Complexity should only be introduced when:
	•	operational value clearly justifies it.
Architecture should remain:
	•	scalable internally, while staying:
	•	simple externally.

Modularity Principle
The MVP should preserve:
	•	modular costing architecture,
	•	deterministic valuation continuity,
	•	and future extensibility.
Even if advanced systems are excluded initially, the architecture should already support future evolution toward:
	•	AI-assisted analytics,
	•	forecasting systems,
	•	dynamic pricing support,
	•	procurement optimization,
	•	and ecosystem-wide operational intelligence.
The MVP should establish:
	•	a stable operational economics foundation, not:
	•	temporary prototype logic.

Deterministic MVP Principle
Critical operational economics behavior must remain deterministic.
Examples:
	•	valuation continuity,
	•	yield-adjusted economics,
	•	overhead allocation,
	•	profitability relationships,
	•	and downstream cost propagation.
The MVP should preserve:
	•	predictable economic behavior,
	•	operational integrity,
	•	and auditability continuity.
