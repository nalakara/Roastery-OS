# AI And Costing Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophical and operational boundaries between AI systems and deterministic costing systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI And Costing Boundaries is to:
- preserve deterministic economic integrity,
- define safe AI operational roles,
- maintain auditability continuity,
- and prevent autonomous manipulation of critical valuation systems.

AI systems may assist:
- operational understanding,
- forecasting,
- optimization,
- and economic analysis.

However:
AI must not replace deterministic operational costing foundations.

This distinction is one of the core trust principles inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats AI as:
- operational intelligence assistance,
not:
- autonomous economic authority.

AI should help operators:
- understand economics,
- analyze profitability,
- identify patterns,
- and improve decision-making.

However:
critical costing relationships must remain:
- deterministic,
- traceable,
- auditable,
- and human-controlled.

Deterministic Boundary Principle

Human Authority Principle

Philosophy Summary
