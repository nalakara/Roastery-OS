# Pricing Relationship

## Purpose

This document defines the relationship between operational costing systems and commercial pricing systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Pricing Relationship is to:
- separate operational valuation from commercial pricing,
- preserve profitability visibility,
- maintain pricing flexibility,
- and define how operational economics support commercial decision-making.

Pricing represents:
- commercial market behavior,
while costing represents:
- operational economic reality.

This distinction is one of the foundational business architecture principles inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats pricing as:
- commercial strategy,
- market positioning,
- and customer-facing economic behavior.

Pricing is not:
- operational valuation,
- inventory cost,
- or manufacturing economics.

Operational costing and commercial pricing are:
- related,
but:
- fundamentally different systems.

The system should preserve:
- clear separation between operational economics
and
- commercial strategy.

	•	explicit,
	•	traceable,

	•	and human-auditable.

Architectural Notes
Pricing Relationship is one of the operational-commercial bridge systems inside the Costing Engine.
Pricing systems influence:
