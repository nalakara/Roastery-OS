# Costing Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines the foundational costing philosophy used across Roastery OS.

The purpose of Costing Philosophy is to:
- establish how operational value evolves,
- preserve transformation-aware valuation continuity,
- maintain profitability visibility,
- and standardize deterministic costing behavior across the ecosystem.

Costing is one of the core operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

Costing exists to explain:
- how inventory value changes,
- why operational economics evolve,
- and how profitability behaves throughout transformation workflows.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats costing as:
- operational value evolution,
not merely:
- accounting calculation,
- bookkeeping,
- or retail pricing infrastructure.

Costing represents:
- the economic behavior of operational transformation.

The system should preserve:
- valuation continuity,
- transformation traceability,
- profitability visibility,
- and deterministic economic relationships.
