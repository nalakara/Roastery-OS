# Cost Auditability

## Purpose

This document defines the cost auditability philosophy and deterministic economic verification behavior used inside the Costing Engine of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Cost Auditability is to:
- preserve economic transparency,
- maintain deterministic valuation continuity,
- support operational verification,
- and ensure traceable profitability relationships throughout the ecosystem.

Cost auditability represents:
- the ability to explain how operational economics evolved over time.

Auditability is one of the foundational trust systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats auditability as:
- operational transparency,
- deterministic economic explainability,
- and transformation-aware verification continuity.

Auditability is not merely:
- accounting compliance,
- enterprise financial bureaucracy,
- or regulatory infrastructure.

Auditability represents:
- operational trustworthiness.

The system should preserve:
- explainable valuation continuity,
- deterministic profitability relationships,
- and traceable operational economics.
