# Multi-Channel Sales

## Purpose

This document defines the multi-channel commerce philosophy and unified sales continuity behavior used inside the POS Engine of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Multi-Channel Sales is to:
- preserve unified commerce continuity,
- maintain centralized inventory integrity,
- support multiple sales ecosystems,
- establish scalable commercial architecture,
- and provide operational visibility across all commerce channels.

Multi-channel systems inside Roastery OS are designed to support:
- operational commerce orchestration,
not merely:
- disconnected sales platforms.

Multi-channel sales is one of the major scalability layers inside the POS Engine.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats multi-channel sales as:
- unified commerce continuity,
- centralized operational sales orchestration,
- and inventory-connected business ecosystems.

Sales channels are not isolated systems.

All channels should preserve:
- inventory continuity,
- pricing continuity,
- transaction continuity,
- customer continuity,
- and operational intelligence continuity.

The system should preserve:
- centralized operational visibility
across:
- distributed commercial experiences.

The MVP Multi-Channel Sales system should prioritize:

	•	lightweight omnichannel readiness,

The MVP intentionally excludes:
	•	enterprise omnichannel ERP,
	•	industrial fulfillment orchestration,
	•	autonomous commerce AI,
	•	and advanced retail infrastructure systems.
