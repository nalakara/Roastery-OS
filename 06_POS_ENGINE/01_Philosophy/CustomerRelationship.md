# Customer Relationship

## Purpose

This document defines the customer relationship philosophy and operational customer continuity behavior used inside the POS Engine of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Customer Relationship is to:
- preserve customer-connected commerce continuity,
- maintain transaction-linked customer visibility,
- support operational customer intelligence,
- establish scalable customer relationship architecture,
- and provide meaningful business relationship continuity.

Customer systems inside Roastery OS are designed to support:
- operational commerce relationships,
not merely:
- contact databases,
- or marketing lists.

Customer relationships are one of the major operational intelligence layers inside the POS Engine.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats customer relationships as:
- operational commerce continuity,
- transaction-connected business relationships,
- and customer behavior visibility systems.

Customers are not merely:
- names in a database,
- payment sources,
- or marketing targets.

Customers represent:
- long-term operational commerce relationships.

The system should preserve:
- transaction continuity,
- purchase continuity,
- inventory relationship continuity,
- and operational customer understanding.
