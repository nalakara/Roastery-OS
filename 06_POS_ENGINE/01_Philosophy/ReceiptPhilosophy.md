# Receipt Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines the receipt philosophy and customer-facing transaction documentation behavior used inside the POS Engine of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Receipt Philosophy is to:
- preserve customer-facing transaction continuity,
- maintain operational commerce traceability,
- support transparent business interactions,
- standardize transaction documentation behavior,
- and provide readable commercial transaction records.

Receipts inside Roastery OS are designed to become:
- operational commerce artifacts,
not merely:
- printed payment slips.

Receipt systems are one of the customer-facing continuity layers inside the POS Engine.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats receipts as:
- customer-facing commerce continuity artifacts,
- transaction traceability documents,
- and operational business transparency systems.

Receipts are not merely:
- printer outputs,
- payment confirmations,
- or mandatory sales paperwork.

Receipts represent:
- readable operational commerce continuity.

The system should preserve:
- transaction readability,
- customer continuity,
- inventory traceability,
- and operational transparency.
