# MVP Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the MVP boundaries and operational scope limitations for the POS Engine inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of MVP Boundaries is to:
- preserve implementation focus,
- maintain operational simplicity,
- avoid premature enterprise complexity,
- and establish a scalable commerce foundation.

The MVP POS Engine should prioritize:
- operational usefulness,
- deterministic commerce continuity,
- and practical specialty coffee business workflows.

The MVP is intended to establish:
- a strong operational commerce foundation,
not:
- a complete enterprise retail ecosystem.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS is designed to evolve progressively.

The MVP POS Engine should therefore focus on:
- operational transaction continuity,
- inventory-connected commerce,
- customer-facing workflows,
- and real-time business visibility.

The MVP should avoid:
- enterprise retail bureaucracy,
- industrial omnichannel complexity,
- and premature automation systems.

The goal is:
- operational usefulness first,
- ecosystem expansion later.

Human-Centered MVP Principle
The MVP should remain:
	•	approachable,
	•	readable,
	•	and operationally practical.
The system should feel:
	•	lightweight,
	•	intuitive,
	•	and useful for real-world coffee business operations.
The MVP should avoid:
	•	ERP intimidation,
	•	corporate retail bureaucracy,
	•	and operational rigidity.

Simplicity Principle
The MVP should prioritize:
	•	workflow clarity,
	•	transaction readability,
	•	and operational continuity.
Complexity should only be introduced when:
	•	operational value clearly justifies it.
Architecture should remain:
	•	scalable internally, while staying:
	•	simple externally.

Operational Visibility Principle
The MVP should already provide:
	•	meaningful real-time operational visibility.
Examples:
Current Revenue
Inventory Consumption
Fast-Moving Products
Customer Activity
Profitability Visibility

The system should help operators:
	•	understand business reality clearly, without:
	•	enterprise dashboard overload.

Modularity Principle
The MVP should preserve:
	•	modular commerce architecture,
	•	future extensibility,
	•	and ecosystem scalability.
Even if advanced systems are excluded initially, the architecture should already support future growth toward:
	•	omnichannel commerce,
	•	AI operational assistance,
	•	advanced forecasting,
	•	subscription ecosystems,
	•	and commerce intelligence systems.
The MVP should establish:
	•	a stable commerce foundation, not:
	•	temporary prototype logic.

Deterministic MVP Principle
Critical commerce behavior must remain deterministic.
Examples:
	•	inventory deduction,
	•	payment continuity,
	•	transaction progression,
	•	profitability visibility,
	•	and refund continuity.
The MVP should preserve:
	•	predictable workflows,
	•	auditability,
	•	and operational integrity.

Sell products confidently

Handle customer transactions

Manage refunds cleanly
Operate across multiple sales channels

without:
	•	spreadsheets,

Architectural Notes
The MVP is not:
	•	a reduced architecture.
The MVP is:
	•	a focused operational slice of a much larger commerce ecosystem.
The architectural foundation should already preserve:
	•	modularity,
	•	deterministic behavior,
	•	transaction continuity,
	•	and operational intelligence readiness.
Future systems should extend:

	•	the commerce foundation.

Long-Term Direction
The MVP is designed to evolve progressively toward:
	•	omnichannel commerce ecosystems,
	•	AI-assisted operational intelligence,
	•	predictive sales forecasting,
	•	subscription orchestration,
	•	and ecosystem-wide business visibility.
However, future complexity should always remain grounded in:
	•	operational usefulness,
	•	human readability,
	•	and deterministic commerce continuity.

Philosophy Summary
The MVP POS Engine is not:
	•	a miniature retail ERP,
	•	or an unfinished enterprise commerce platform.
The MVP POS Engine is:
	•	a focused operational commerce foundation,
	•	designed to solve real specialty coffee business workflows,
	•	while preserving long-term ecosystem scalability.
The MVP should feel:
	•	simple externally, but:
	•	architecturally powerful internally.
