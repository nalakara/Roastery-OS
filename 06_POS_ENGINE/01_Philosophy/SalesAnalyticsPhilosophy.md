# Sales Analytics Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines the sales analytics philosophy and operational commerce intelligence behavior used inside the POS Engine of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Sales Analytics Philosophy is to:
- preserve operational business visibility,
- maintain commerce intelligence continuity,
- support data-informed decision making,
- establish scalable analytics architecture,
- and provide meaningful operational insights.

Sales analytics inside Roastery OS are designed to support:
- operational intelligence,
not merely:
- historical reporting,
- or dashboard visualization.

Sales analytics are one of the foundational intelligence layers inside the POS Engine.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats sales analytics as:
- operational commerce intelligence,
- business behavior visibility,
- and future operational forecasting infrastructure.

Analytics are not merely:
- charts,
- spreadsheets,
- or management reports.

Analytics represent:
- interpretable operational business behavior.

The system should preserve:
- transaction continuity,
- inventory continuity,
- profitability continuity,
- and customer continuity.
