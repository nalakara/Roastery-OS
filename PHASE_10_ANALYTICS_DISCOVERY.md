## 3. Operational Questions Answered

### A. Inventory
- **What physical stock exists?**  
  Answered via `GET /api/analytics/inventory` (total lots, quantity on-hand by material category and unit).
- **What is available vs reserved?**  
  Answered via `quantity_on_hand - reserved_quantity = availableQuantity`.
- **Which lots are aging?**  
  Answered via `ageDays` calculated from `receivedAt` to current timestamp.
- **Which lots are depleted / partially consumed?**  
  Answered via `lotState` (`ACTIVE`, `DEPLETED`, `QUARANTINED`).

### B. Production & Yield
- **How much has been roasted, blended, and packaged?**  
  Answered via `GET /api/analytics/transformations` grouped by `archetype` (`ROASTING`, `BLENDING`, `ASSEMBLY_PACKAGING`).
- **What are actual yields and where are losses occurring?**  
  Answered via `yieldRatio` (e.g. 83.33% output yield on roasting) and `unrecoverableWasteQuantity` (`WASTE-CHAFF-MOISTURE`).

### C. Cost & Economic Absorption
- **What is the current HPP of produced lots?**  
  Answered via `GET /api/analytics/production-cost` displaying `materialCost` + `conversionCost` = `totalLotCost` and `unitCost` per UOM.
- **Which transformations contribute the most conversion cost?**  
  Answered via `directConversionCost` derived from `cost_event` records.

### D. Commercial & Profitability
- **What has been sold and through which channel?**  
  Answered via `GET /api/analytics/commercial` separating `RETAIL_POS` vs `WHOLESALE_CONTRACT`.
- **Which SKUs move most?**  
  Answered via `totalUnitsSold` per SKU in `skuSummaries`.
- **What is revenue vs COGS vs gross margin?**  
  Answered via authoritative `grand_total` revenue minus `cogs_record` total = `grossMarginAmount` and `grossMarginPercentage`.

### E. Supplier Contribution
- **Which suppliers have supplied which materials and what acquisition spend occurred?**  
  Answered via `GET /api/analytics/suppliers` listing total spend and material quantities by supplier.

## 4. Supported vs Unsupported Metrics

### Supported Metrics (Available Now)
1. Live inventory position, total valuation, and available vs reserved balance.
2. Lot aging in days since receipt.
3. Transformation yield ratio and loss quantity (for dimensionally compatible units).
4. Direct conversion costs and full absorption HPP of produced lots.
5. Sales revenue, COGS, and gross margin by sales channel and SKU.
6. Supplier procurement spend and volume breakdown.
7. Bi-directional lineage drilldown from any lot.

### Unsupported Metrics (Documented Data Gaps)
1. **Machine Telemetry & Roast Profile Curves**:  
   `batch.process_telemetry` stores JSON data, but real-time sensor streams and roast profile time-series curves are not yet ingested.
2. **Forecasted Demand / Predictive Sales**:  
   Requires historic time-series data across consecutive quarters.
3. **Inventory Reorder Level / Safety Stock Warnings**:  
   Requires a min/max stock rule configuration table (not part of current transactional schema).
4. **Machine Downtime / OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)**:  
   Requires equipment maintenance and downtime event logs.

### Endpoints
- `GET /api/analytics/inventory`: Physical inventory position, reserved vs available, aging, and valuation.
- `GET /api/analytics/transformations?filter=ALL|TODAY|LAST_7_DAYS|THIS_MONTH`: Physical yields, waste, and conversion costs.
- `GET /api/analytics/production-cost`: Absorption HPP breakdown of produced lots.
- `GET /api/analytics/commercial?filter=ALL|TODAY|LAST_7_DAYS|THIS_MONTH`: Revenue, COGS, and gross margin across POS and Wholesale.
- `GET /api/analytics/suppliers?filter=ALL|TODAY|LAST_7_DAYS|THIS_MONTH`: Procurement receipts and spend.
- `GET /api/analytics/summary?filter=ALL|TODAY|LAST_7_DAYS|THIS_MONTH`: High-level instrument panel summary.

## 6. Guardrails Compliance Verification

1. **Analytics is read-only**: All endpoints and service methods are strictly read-only (`SELECT` queries).
2. **Analytics does not own domain state**: Reads directly from authoritative tables (`inventory_lot`, `transformation`, `lot_valuation_record`, `commercial_order`, `purchase_receipt`).
3. **No shadow inventory, costing, or lineage**: 100% derived on demand.
4. **No data warehouse, ETL, or event streaming**: Implemented directly within the modular monolith.
5. **No AI / Predictive fabrications**: Pure factual reporting of actual operational events.
6. **Physical UOM Dimension Safety**: Never sums incompatible physical units (mass vs count).
7. **Strict Tenant Isolation**: All queries enforce `organization_id = $1`.

## 7. Product & Domain Discoveries

1. **Acyclic COGS Record Strength**:  
   Because COGS is realized as an immutable snapshot (`cogs_record`) linked to `fulfillment_allocation`, gross margin calculations for POS and Wholesale are fast and mathematically exact, avoiding expensive real-time graph re-traversals during commercial reporting.
2. **Yield Reporting Clarity**:  
   Comparing `primary_quantity_consumed` vs `primary_quantity_produced` on roasting transformations provides immediate visibility into green-to-roasted shrinkage (e.g. 16.67% loss on Flores roast).
3. **Cross-Module Drilldown Utility**:  
   Allowing an operator to click **"Silsilah &rarr;"** on any analytics lot instantly opens the Bi-directional Traceability Explorer, connecting high-level economic numbers directly back to farm origin receipts.
