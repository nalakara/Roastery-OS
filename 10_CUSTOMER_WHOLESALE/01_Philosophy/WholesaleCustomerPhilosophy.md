# Wholesale Customer Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines the foundational philosophy and operational continuity principles governing wholesale customers inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Wholesale Customer Philosophy is to:
- preserve continuity-aware customer relationships,
- maintain operational demand visibility,
- support long-term ecosystem stability,
- and establish human-centered business continuity intelligence.

Wholesale customers are treated as:
- operational continuity partners,
not merely:
- transactional buyers.

This philosophy is foundational to:
- forecasting,
- production planning,
- procurement preparedness,
- profitability continuity,
- and ecosystem operational stability.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats wholesale customers as:
- recurring operational continuity entities.

Wholesale customers influence:
- production scheduling,
- inventory allocation,
- procurement planning,
- operational preparedness,
- and long-term business continuity.

The system should preserve:
- relationship continuity,
- demand visibility,
- operational predictability,
- and ecosystem explainability.

Wholesale relationships should remain:
- understandable,
- traceable,
- and operationally meaningful.
