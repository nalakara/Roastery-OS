# Production Planning Relationship

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational continuity principles governing the relationship between wholesale customer demand and production planning inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Production Planning Relationship is to:
- preserve continuity-aware production preparedness,
- maintain explainable operational planning visibility,
- support deterministic workflow stability,
- and establish safe coordination between customer demand and production continuity.

Production planning is treated as:
- operational preparedness infrastructure,
not merely:
- manufacturing scheduling.

This philosophy is foundational to:
- roasting workflows,
- blending continuity,
- packaging preparedness,
- inventory allocation,
- procurement continuity,
- and ecosystem operational stability.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats production planning as:
- continuity-aware operational coordination.

Production planning should help operators:
- align demand continuity with workflow capacity,
- prepare inventory and procurement,
- stabilize operational throughput,
- and maintain ecosystem continuity integrity.

The system should preserve:
- explainable production planning,
- operational preparedness visibility,
- deterministic workflow continuity,
- and human-centered operational understanding.

Production planning should remain:
- operationally grounded,
- traceable,
- and continuity-aware.
