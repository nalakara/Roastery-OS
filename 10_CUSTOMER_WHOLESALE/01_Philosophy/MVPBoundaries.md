# MVP Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the MVP scope, implementation limitations, and architectural boundaries for the Customer Wholesale module inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of MVP Boundaries is to:
- preserve continuity-aware customer relationship infrastructure,
- maintain operationally understandable wholesale systems,
- avoid premature enterprise CRM complexity,
- and establish a scalable business continuity foundation.

The MVP Customer Wholesale module should prioritize:
- recurring customer continuity visibility,
- operational preparedness support,
- forecasting relationships,
- production planning alignment,
- and explainable customer intelligence.

The MVP is intended to establish:
- operational relationship continuity infrastructure,
not:
- enterprise customer automation systems.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS evolves progressively.

The MVP Customer Wholesale module should therefore focus on:
- practical operational continuity,
- explainable customer intelligence,
- relationship stability visibility,
- and preparedness-aware operational planning.

The MVP should avoid:
- enterprise CRM bureaucracy,
- manipulative customer optimization systems,
- and autonomous AI-driven customer governance.

The goal is:
- operational usefulness first,
- ecosystem sophistication later.
