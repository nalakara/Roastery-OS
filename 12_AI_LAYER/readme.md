
# AI Layer

## Purpose

AI Layer defines the operational intelligence, probabilistic reasoning, and human-assistive AI architecture used across Roastery OS.

This module acts as:
- the ecosystem intelligence layer,
- operational cognition infrastructure,
- and AI-assisted interpretation system
for all operational continuity workflows.

AI Layer standardizes:
- how AI interacts with operational continuity,
- how probabilistic intelligence is separated from deterministic truth,
- and how AI systems remain explainable, traceable, and human-centered throughout the ecosystem.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats AI as:
- operational intelligence assistance,
not:
- autonomous operational authority.

AI should help operators:
- understand operational continuity,
- analyze workflow behavior,
- identify operational anomalies,
- improve preparedness,
- and support ecosystem cognition.

However:
critical operational continuity must remain:
- deterministic,
- explicit,
- traceable,
- auditable,
- and human-controlled.

AI workflows should remain:
- explainable,
- continuity-aware,
- modular,
- and human-centered.
