# AI Workflow Integration

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and architectural principles governing how AI systems integrate with operational workflows throughout Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI Workflow Integration is to:
- preserve deterministic operational continuity,
- maintain explainable AI-assisted workflow intelligence,
- support human-centered operational cognition,
- and prevent uncontrolled AI-driven workflow mutation.

AI systems should integrate as:
- operational cognition layers,
not:
- autonomous workflow controllers.

This distinction preserves:
- operational trust,
- ecosystem integrity,
- auditability,
- and continuity explainability.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats workflow integration as:
- continuity-aware AI augmentation.

AI systems should:
- observe workflows,
- analyze continuity,
- identify patterns,
- generate recommendations,
- support preparedness,
- and assist operational understanding.

AI systems should not autonomously:
- control workflows,
- mutate operational continuity,
- or redefine operational truth.

The system should preserve:
- deterministic workflow integrity,
- explainable AI interaction,
- continuity-aware integration,
- and human operational authority.
