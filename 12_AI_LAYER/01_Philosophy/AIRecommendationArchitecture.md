# AI Recommendation Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and architectural principles of AI recommendation systems used throughout Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI Recommendation Architecture is to:
- preserve explainable recommendation intelligence,
- maintain deterministic operational continuity integrity,
- support human-centered operational cognition,
- and establish safe advisory AI behavior across the ecosystem.

AI recommendations should help operators:
- understand operational risks,
- improve preparedness,
- identify optimization opportunities,
- and support operational decision-making.

AI recommendations should remain:
- advisory,
not:
- autonomous operational execution.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats recommendations as:
- operational cognition assistance,
not:
- autonomous operational authority.

Recommendations should help humans:
- interpret operational continuity,
- evaluate ecosystem behavior,
- identify potential risks,
- and improve workflow awareness.

The system should preserve:
- explainable recommendation intelligence,
- continuity-aware reasoning,
- deterministic operational integrity,
- and human-readable operational guidance.
