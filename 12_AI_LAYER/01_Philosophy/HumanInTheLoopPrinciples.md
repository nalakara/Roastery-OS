# Human In The Loop Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the human-in-the-loop philosophy and operational authority principles used throughout the AI Layer inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Human In The Loop Principles is to:
- preserve human operational authority,
- maintain deterministic operational integrity,
- support explainable AI-assisted cognition,
- and prevent uncontrolled autonomous operational mutation.

Human-in-the-loop architecture ensures that:
- AI assists operations,
while:
- humans remain the final operational authority.

This distinction is one of the foundational operational trust principles inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats humans as:
- operational decision authorities.

AI systems exist to:
- augment operational cognition,
not:
- replace operational judgment.

Humans should remain responsible for:
- validating operational interpretation,
- approving critical decisions,
- understanding continuity relationships,
- and maintaining ecosystem integrity.

The system should preserve:
- human authority,
- operational explainability,
- deterministic continuity,
- and ecosystem trust.
