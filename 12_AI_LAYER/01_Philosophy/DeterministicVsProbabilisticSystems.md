# Deterministic Vs Probabilistic Systems

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophical and architectural distinction between deterministic systems and probabilistic systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Deterministic Vs Probabilistic Systems is to:
- preserve operational truth integrity,
- maintain explainable AI-assisted cognition,
- protect deterministic continuity relationships,
- and establish safe interaction boundaries between operational systems and AI systems.

This distinction is one of the foundational architectural principles inside Roastery OS.

---
