# MVP Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the MVP scope, implementation limitations, and architectural boundaries for the AI Layer inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of MVP Boundaries is to:
- preserve deterministic operational continuity integrity,
- maintain explainable AI-assisted cognition,
- avoid premature autonomous AI complexity,
- and establish a scalable operational intelligence foundation.

The MVP AI Layer should prioritize:
- operational cognition assistance,
- preparedness intelligence,
- continuity-aware interpretation,
- explainable recommendations,
- and human-centered operational augmentation.

The MVP is intended to establish:
- safe operational intelligence infrastructure,
not:
- autonomous AI governance systems.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS evolves progressively.

The MVP AI Layer should therefore focus on:
- practical operational intelligence,
- explainable ecosystem cognition,
- continuity-aware interpretation,
- and deterministic operational protection.

The MVP should avoid:
- autonomous ecosystem governance,
- opaque AI behavior,
- and premature AI-native operational orchestration.

The goal is:
- operational usefulness first,
- AI sophistication later.
