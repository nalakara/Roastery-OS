# AI Permission Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the operational and architectural permission boundaries governing AI behavior throughout Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI Permission Boundaries is to:
- preserve deterministic operational continuity integrity,
- maintain human operational authority,
- support safe AI-assisted cognition,
- and prevent uncontrolled autonomous ecosystem mutation.

AI systems may:
- analyze operational continuity,
- identify patterns,
- generate recommendations,
- support forecasting,
- and assist operational understanding.

However:
AI systems must operate within explicit operational permission boundaries.

This distinction is one of the foundational operational trust principles inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats AI as:
- operational cognition assistance,
not:
- autonomous operational authority.

AI should:
- interpret,
- analyze,
- recommend,
- summarize,
- and support preparedness visibility.

AI should not autonomously:
- mutate deterministic operational continuity,
- redefine operational truth,
- or execute operational actions without human governance.

The system should preserve:
- explicit operational permissions,
- deterministic continuity integrity,
- explainable AI behavior,
- and human operational authority.
