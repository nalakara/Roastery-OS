# AI Explainability Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational principles of explainability used throughout the AI Layer inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI Explainability Principles is to:
- preserve transparent operational intelligence,
- maintain explainable AI-assisted cognition,
- support human operational understanding,
- and prevent black-box ecosystem behavior.

Explainability ensures that:
- operators can understand how AI arrived at interpretations,
recommendations,
forecasts,
and anomaly visibility.

Explainability is one of the foundational operational trust systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats explainability as:
- operational visibility continuity.

AI systems should help operators understand:
- why a recommendation exists,
- why a forecast was generated,
- why an anomaly was detected,
- and what operational continuity influenced interpretation.

The system should preserve:
- transparent reasoning visibility,
- continuity-aware interpretation,
- deterministic operational integrity,
- and human-readable operational cognition.
