# Supplier Entity Structure

## Purpose

This document defines the foundational supplier entity structure and operational sourcing identity behavior used inside the Supplier System of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Supplier Entity Structure is to:
- preserve deterministic supplier identity,
- maintain sourcing continuity,
- support procurement explainability,
- and standardize how supplier entities are represented throughout the ecosystem.

Supplier entities represent:
- operational sourcing continuity,
not merely:
- vendor records,
- procurement contacts,
- or purchasing references.

Supplier entity structure is one of the foundational upstream continuity systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats supplier entities as:
- operational sourcing identities,
- upstream continuity anchors,
- and procurement relationship infrastructure.

Supplier entities are not merely:
- contact databases,
- accounting vendor lists,
- or purchasing references.

Supplier entities represent:
- operational sourcing continuity.

The system should preserve:
- deterministic supplier identity,
- sourcing continuity,
- and operational explainability.
