# Supplier Risk And Reliability

## Purpose

This document defines the supplier risk and reliability philosophy and sourcing resilience behavior used inside the Supplier System of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Supplier Risk And Reliability is to:
- preserve operational sourcing stability visibility,
- maintain explainable procurement resilience,
- support continuity risk awareness,
- and define how supplier reliability affects ecosystem continuity throughout the system.

Supplier risk and reliability represent:
- operational sourcing resilience continuity,
not merely:
- vendor scoring,
- procurement warnings,
- or purchasing evaluation.

Supplier reliability visibility is one of the foundational sourcing resilience systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats supplier reliability as:
- operational continuity stability,
not merely:
- procurement performance scoring.

Supplier reliability directly affects:
- inventory continuity,
- operational stability,
- product consistency,
- costing predictability,
- and downstream customer experience.

The system should preserve:
- deterministic sourcing reliability visibility,
- operational risk explainability,
- and continuity resilience awareness.
