# Supplier Pricing Continuity

## Purpose

This document defines the supplier pricing continuity philosophy and operational economic relationship behavior used inside the Supplier System of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Supplier Pricing Continuity is to:
- preserve explainable sourcing economics,
- maintain operational costing continuity,
- support procurement pricing visibility,
- and define how supplier pricing affects downstream operational continuity throughout the ecosystem.

Supplier pricing represents:
- operational economic continuity,
not merely:
- vendor price lists,
- procurement negotiation outcomes,
- or accounting values.

Supplier pricing continuity is one of the foundational sourcing intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats supplier pricing as:
- operational continuity economics,
not merely:
- purchasing cost data.

Supplier pricing directly affects:
- inventory valuation,
- operational costing,
- profitability,
- procurement resilience,
- and downstream pricing continuity.

The system should preserve:
- deterministic pricing continuity,
- sourcing explainability,
- and operational economic visibility.
