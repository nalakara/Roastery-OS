# Supplier Traceability

## Purpose

This document defines the supplier traceability philosophy and upstream sourcing lineage behavior used inside the Supplier System of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Supplier Traceability is to:
- preserve upstream sourcing continuity,
- maintain explainable material ancestry,
- support operational procurement genealogy,
- and define how supplier-origin relationships remain traceable throughout the ecosystem.

Supplier traceability represents:
- upstream operational lineage continuity.

Supplier traceability is one of the foundational sourcing continuity systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats supplier traceability as:
- operational sourcing lineage continuity,
not merely:
- procurement history,
- vendor documentation,
- or purchasing records.

Supplier relationships influence:
- inventory ancestry,
- operational continuity,
- costing explainability,
- product consistency,
- and downstream genealogy.

The system should preserve:
- deterministic sourcing lineage,
- operational ancestry continuity,
- and explainable procurement genealogy.
