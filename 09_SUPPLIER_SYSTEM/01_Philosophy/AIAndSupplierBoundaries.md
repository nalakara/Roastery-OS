# AI And Supplier Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophical and operational boundaries between AI systems and supplier continuity systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI And Supplier Boundaries is to:
- preserve deterministic sourcing continuity,
- maintain operational procurement trust,
- support explainable supplier intelligence,
- and prevent autonomous mutation of critical sourcing relationships.

AI systems may assist:
- supplier analytics,
- procurement forecasting,
- sourcing optimization,
- risk detection,
- and operational intelligence.

However:
AI must not replace deterministic supplier continuity foundations.

This distinction is one of the core operational trust principles inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats AI as:
- operational intelligence assistance,
not:
- autonomous sourcing authority.

AI should help operators:
- understand sourcing continuity,
- analyze procurement behavior,
- identify operational anomalies,
- and improve procurement awareness.

However:
critical supplier continuity must remain:
- deterministic,
- explicit,
- traceable,
- auditable,
- and human-controlled.
