# Supplier Performance Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the supplier performance philosophy and operational continuity evaluation principles used inside the Supplier System of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Supplier Performance Principles is to:
- preserve sourcing quality visibility,
- maintain operational supplier evaluation continuity,
- support procurement explainability,
- and define how supplier performance affects ecosystem continuity throughout the system.

Supplier performance represents:
- operational continuity quality,
not merely:
- vendor scoring,
- procurement ranking,
- or purchasing evaluation.

Supplier performance visibility is one of the foundational sourcing intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats supplier performance as:
- operational continuity behavior,
not merely:
- procurement metrics.

Supplier performance directly affects:
- inventory consistency,
- product quality,
- operational reliability,
- costing stability,
- and downstream customer experience.

The system should preserve:
- deterministic performance visibility,
- sourcing explainability,
- and operational continuity intelligence.
