# Supplier Relationship Model

## Purpose

This document defines the supplier relationship model and upstream operational continuity behavior used inside the Supplier System of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Supplier Relationship Model is to:
- preserve deterministic sourcing relationships,
- maintain operational continuity between suppliers and workflows,
- support procurement explainability,
- and define how supplier relationships evolve throughout the ecosystem.

Supplier relationships represent:
- operational sourcing continuity,
not merely:
- transactional procurement activity,
- purchasing interactions,
- or vendor communication records.

Supplier relationship modeling is one of the foundational upstream continuity systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats supplier relationships as:
- long-term operational continuity relationships,
not merely:
- procurement transactions.

Supplier relationships influence:
- inventory continuity,
- operational stability,
- product consistency,
- sourcing resilience,
- and downstream traceability.

The system should preserve:
- deterministic relationship continuity,
- sourcing explainability,
- operational trust,
- and procurement genealogy visibility.
