# Blend Logging

## Purpose

This document defines the blend logging philosophy and operational logging behavior used inside the Blend Engine of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Blend Logging is to:
- preserve blend production history,
- maintain operational visibility,
- support composition continuity,
- provide readable production records,
- and create long-term production intelligence infrastructure.

Blend logging acts as:
- operational memory,
- composition history preservation,
- and transformation storytelling infrastructure.

Blend logging is not merely:
- note storage,
- or administrative record keeping.

Blend logging preserves how blend production operationally happened.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats blend logging as:
- operational history preservation,
- production visibility infrastructure,
- and transformation documentation.

Blend logging is not merely:
- recipe journaling,
- or workflow note-taking.

Blend logging represents:
- observable production behavior,
- operational transformation context,
- and composition evolution history.

The system should preserve blend history in:
- a readable,
- traceable,
- and operationally meaningful way.

Critical production history must remain:

	•	and human-auditable.

MVP Scope
The MVP Blend Logging system should prioritize:
	•	BlendBatch history,
	•	composition visibility,
	•	transformation continuity,
	•	yield logging,
	•	and operational timeline preservation.

	•	industrial telemetry systems,
	•	automated production monitoring,
	•	advanced manufacturing orchestration,
