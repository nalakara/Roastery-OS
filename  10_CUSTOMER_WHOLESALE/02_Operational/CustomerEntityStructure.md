# Customer Entity Structure

## Purpose

This document defines the operational entity structure and continuity relationships for wholesale customers inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Customer Entity Structure is to:
- preserve deterministic customer continuity,
- maintain explainable relationship visibility,
- support operational planning workflows,
- and establish scalable business continuity architecture.

Customer entities are designed to represent:
- operational relationship continuity,
not merely:
- transactional customer records.

The architecture should preserve:
- recurring demand visibility,
- operational dependencies,
- relationship continuity,
- and ecosystem traceability.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats customer entities as:
- operational continuity entities.

Customer entities should preserve:
- relationship history,
- operational patterns,
- pricing continuity,
- forecasting signals,
- production influence,
- and preparedness visibility.

Customer structures should remain:
- deterministic,
- explainable,
- traceable,
- and operationally meaningful.
