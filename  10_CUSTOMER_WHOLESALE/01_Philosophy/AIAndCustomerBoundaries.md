# AI And Customer Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophical and operational boundaries governing AI interaction with wholesale customer systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI And Customer Boundaries is to:
- preserve deterministic customer continuity integrity,
- maintain explainable AI-assisted relationship intelligence,
- support human-centered business governance,
- and prevent manipulative or autonomous AI-controlled customer behavior systems.

AI systems may:
- analyze customer continuity,
- identify operational patterns,
- support preparedness visibility,
- generate recommendations,
- and assist operational understanding.

However:
AI systems must not autonomously:
- govern customer relationships,
- manipulate customer behavior,
- redefine operational truth,
- or mutate continuity relationships without human oversight.

This distinction is foundational to:
- operational trust,
- relationship integrity,
- and ecosystem continuity safety.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats AI as:
- operational cognition assistance,
not:
- autonomous relationship governance.

AI systems should help operators:
- understand customer continuity,
- identify preparedness risks,
- improve operational visibility,
- and support continuity-aware decision-making.

AI systems should not:
- manipulate relationships,
- autonomously execute customer strategies,
- or redefine business continuity.

The system should preserve:
- explainable AI behavior,
- deterministic relationship continuity,
- operational transparency,
- and human operational authority.
