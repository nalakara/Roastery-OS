# Relationship Continuity

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational continuity principles governing long-term wholesale customer relationships inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Relationship Continuity is to:
- preserve recurring operational relationship visibility,
- maintain continuity-aware business intelligence,
- support long-term ecosystem stability,
- and establish explainable customer continuity infrastructure.

Relationship continuity ensures that:
- wholesale customers are understood as evolving operational relationships,
not merely:
- isolated transactions.

This philosophy is foundational to:
- forecasting,
- production planning,
- procurement preparedness,
- profitability continuity,
- and ecosystem operational resilience.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats customer relationships as:
- continuity systems.

Wholesale relationships evolve through:
- recurring orders,
- operational coordination,
- pricing continuity,
- forecasting behavior,
- communication continuity,
- and business trust development.

The system should preserve:
- relationship history,
- continuity visibility,
- operational predictability,
- and ecosystem explainability.

Relationship continuity should remain:
- deterministic,
- traceable,
- understandable,
- and operationally meaningful.
