# Customer Pricing Logic

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational continuity principles governing wholesale customer pricing systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Customer Pricing Logic is to:
- preserve continuity-aware pricing relationships,
- maintain explainable profitability visibility,
- support long-term operational sustainability,
- and establish deterministic pricing continuity throughout the ecosystem.

Pricing is treated as:
- relationship continuity infrastructure,
not merely:
- transactional numerical calculation.

This philosophy is foundational to:
- profitability continuity,
- production planning,
- procurement preparedness,
- customer relationship stability,
- and ecosystem operational resilience.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats wholesale pricing as:
- continuity-aware relationship logic.

Pricing systems should help operators:
- maintain long-term customer stability,
- preserve profitability continuity,
- support operational predictability,
- and align pricing with ecosystem sustainability.

The system should preserve:
- explainable pricing continuity,
- operational transparency,
- deterministic pricing relationships,
- and human-centered business understanding.

Pricing continuity should remain:
- traceable,
- explainable,
- and operationally meaningful.
