# Demand Forecast Relationship

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational continuity principles governing the relationship between wholesale customer demand and forecasting systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Demand Forecast Relationship is to:
- preserve explainable demand forecasting continuity,
- maintain operational preparedness visibility,
- support continuity-aware planning systems,
- and establish safe forecasting intelligence across the ecosystem.

Demand forecasting is treated as:
- preparedness intelligence infrastructure,
not:
- deterministic operational truth.

This philosophy is foundational to:
- procurement planning,
- production scheduling,
- inventory preparedness,
- profitability continuity,
- and ecosystem operational resilience.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats demand forecasting as:
- continuity-aware preparedness intelligence.

Forecasting systems should help operators:
- understand recurring demand behavior,
- identify operational risks,
- improve preparedness visibility,
- and support operational planning decisions.

Forecasting systems should preserve:
- explainability,
- continuity awareness,
- probabilistic interpretation,
- and human-centered operational understanding.

Demand forecasting should remain:
- advisory,
not:
- autonomous operational governance.
