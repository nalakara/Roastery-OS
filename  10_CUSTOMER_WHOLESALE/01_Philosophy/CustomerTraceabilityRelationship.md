# Customer Traceability Relationship

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational continuity principles governing the relationship between wholesale customers and traceability systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of Customer Traceability Relationship is to:
- preserve downstream continuity visibility,
- maintain explainable customer allocation traceability,
- support operational accountability,
- and establish continuity-aware fulfillment transparency throughout the ecosystem.

Customer traceability is treated as:
- operational continuity infrastructure,
not merely:
- delivery logging or shipment tracking.

This philosophy is foundational to:
- batch genealogy,
- fulfillment continuity,
- recall preparedness,
- production allocation visibility,
- and ecosystem operational trust.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats customer traceability as:
- downstream operational continuity visibility.

Customer relationships extend beyond:
- order fulfillment.

They also preserve:
- where products flowed,
- how batches were allocated,
- what operational continuity was affected,
- and how ecosystem accountability remains explainable.

The system should preserve:
- deterministic allocation continuity,
- explainable fulfillment visibility,
- operational accountability,
- and human-centered traceability understanding.

Customer traceability should remain:
- explicit,
- traceable,
- auditable,
- and operationally meaningful.
