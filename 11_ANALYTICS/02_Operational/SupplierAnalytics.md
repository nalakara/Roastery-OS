# Supplier Analytics

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational intelligence principles of supplier analytics used inside the Analytics system of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Supplier Analytics is to:
- preserve explainable sourcing continuity visibility,
- maintain supplier intelligence awareness,
- support procurement resilience understanding,
- and define how supplier behavior becomes understandable throughout the ecosystem.

Supplier analytics represents:
- sourcing continuity intelligence,
not merely:
- vendor reporting,
- procurement scoring,
- or supplier dashboards.

Supplier analytics is one of the foundational operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats supplier analytics as:
- sourcing continuity visibility,
not merely:
- vendor evaluation.

Supplier behavior directly affects:
- inventory continuity,
- production stability,
- operational costing,
- workflow resilience,
- and customer experience continuity.

The system should preserve:
- continuity-aware supplier visibility,
- deterministic sourcing context,
- explainable procurement intelligence,
- and human-readable supplier understanding.
