# Cost Analytics

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational intelligence principles of cost analytics used inside the Analytics system of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Cost Analytics is to:
- preserve explainable operational economic visibility,
- maintain continuity-aware costing intelligence,
- support profitability understanding,
- and define how operational economics become understandable throughout the ecosystem.

Cost analytics represents:
- operational economic intelligence,
not merely:
- accounting reports,
- financial summaries,
- or profitability dashboards.

Cost analytics is one of the foundational operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats cost analytics as:
- operational economic continuity visibility,
not merely:
- financial reporting.

Cost behavior directly affects:
- inventory continuity,
- procurement stability,
- production sustainability,
- profitability,
- and operational resilience.

The system should preserve:
- continuity-aware costing visibility,
- deterministic economic context,
- explainable profitability intelligence,
- and human-readable operational economics.
