# Operational Anomaly Detection

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational intelligence principles of anomaly detection used inside the Analytics system of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Operational Anomaly Detection is to:
- preserve explainable operational irregularity visibility,
- maintain continuity-aware anomaly awareness,
- support operational preparedness,
- and define how unusual ecosystem behavior becomes understandable throughout the system.

Operational anomaly detection represents:
- operational continuity awareness,
not merely:
- alert systems,
- statistical outlier detection,
- or automated warning infrastructure.

Anomaly detection is one of the foundational operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats anomaly detection as:
- operational irregularity visibility,
not merely:
- error detection.

Anomalies directly affect:
- workflow stability,
- operational continuity,
- sourcing resilience,
- profitability continuity,
- and ecosystem trust.

The system should preserve:
- explainable anomaly visibility,
- continuity-aware irregularity context,
- deterministic operational understanding,
- and human-readable anomaly intelligence.
