# Production Analytics

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational intelligence principles of production analytics used inside the Analytics system of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Production Analytics is to:
- preserve explainable production continuity visibility,
- maintain workflow intelligence awareness,
- support operational stability understanding,
- and define how production behavior becomes understandable throughout the ecosystem.

Production analytics represents:
- operational workflow intelligence,
not merely:
- manufacturing reporting,
- production statistics,
- or output dashboards.

Production analytics is one of the foundational operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats production analytics as:
- operational continuity visibility,
not merely:
- production measurement.

Production behavior directly affects:
- inventory continuity,
- workflow stability,
- product consistency,
- operational costing,
- and customer experience continuity.

The system should preserve:
- continuity-aware production visibility,
- deterministic workflow context,
- explainable operational intelligence,
- and human-readable production understanding.
