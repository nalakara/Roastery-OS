# Traceability Analytics

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational intelligence principles of traceability analytics used inside the Analytics system of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Traceability Analytics is to:
- preserve explainable operational genealogy visibility,
- maintain continuity-aware ancestry intelligence,
- support operational auditability,
- and define how ecosystem lineage becomes understandable throughout the system.

Traceability analytics represents:
- operational continuity intelligence,
not merely:
- batch lookup,
- inventory tracking,
- or audit reporting.

Traceability analytics is one of the foundational operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats traceability analytics as:
- operational genealogy visibility,
not merely:
- tracking infrastructure.

Traceability directly affects:
- operational explainability,
- sourcing continuity,
- workflow visibility,
- recall capability,
- and ecosystem trust continuity.

The system should preserve:
- continuity-aware lineage visibility,
- deterministic genealogy context,
- explainable operational ancestry,
- and human-readable continuity understanding.
