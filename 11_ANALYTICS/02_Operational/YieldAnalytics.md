# Yield Analytics

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational intelligence principles of yield analytics used inside the Analytics system of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Yield Analytics is to:
- preserve explainable yield continuity visibility,
- maintain operational transformation awareness,
- support costing intelligence,
- and define how yield behavior becomes understandable throughout the ecosystem.

Yield analytics represents:
- operational transformation intelligence,
not merely:
- percentage calculations,
- shrinkage reporting,
- or production efficiency metrics.

Yield analytics is one of the foundational operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats yield analytics as:
- operational transformation visibility,
not merely:
- output measurement.

Yield behavior directly affects:
- inventory continuity,
- operational costing,
- product consistency,
- procurement planning,
- and profitability continuity.

The system should preserve:
- continuity-aware yield visibility,
- deterministic transformation context,
- explainable operational intelligence,
- and human-readable yield understanding.
