# MVP Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the MVP boundaries and implementation scope limitations for the Analytics system inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of MVP Boundaries is to:
- preserve architectural focus,
- maintain operational realism,
- avoid premature enterprise BI complexity,
- and establish a scalable operational intelligence foundation.

The MVP Analytics system should prioritize:
- explainable operational visibility,
- continuity-aware intelligence,
- human-readable ecosystem understanding,
- operational preparedness,
- and deterministic analytics continuity.

The MVP is intended to establish:
- a strong operational intelligence foundation,
not:
- enterprise AI governance infrastructure.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS evolves progressively.

The MVP Analytics system should therefore focus on:
- practical operational visibility,
- explainable ecosystem intelligence,
- operational continuity awareness,
- and human-centered analytics.

The MVP should avoid:
- enterprise BI bureaucracy,
- black-box AI complexity,
- and premature autonomous operational governance.

The goal is:
- operational usefulness first,
- ecosystem sophistication later.
