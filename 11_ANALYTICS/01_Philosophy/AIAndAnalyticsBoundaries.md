# AI And Analytics Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophical and operational boundaries between AI systems and analytics continuity systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI And Analytics Boundaries is to:
- preserve deterministic operational continuity,
- maintain explainable analytics intelligence,
- support human-centered operational cognition,
- and prevent autonomous mutation of critical ecosystem relationships.

AI systems may assist:
- operational analysis,
- anomaly detection,
- forecasting,
- pattern recognition,
- and ecosystem intelligence.

However:
AI must not replace deterministic operational continuity foundations.

This distinction is one of the core operational trust principles inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats AI as:
- operational intelligence assistance,
not:
- autonomous operational authority.

AI should help operators:
- understand ecosystem continuity,
- identify workflow patterns,
- analyze operational anomalies,
- and improve preparedness awareness.

However:
critical operational continuity must remain:
- deterministic,
- explicit,
- traceable,
- auditable,
- and human-controlled.
