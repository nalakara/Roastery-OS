# Forecasting Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy and operational intelligence principles of forecasting systems used inside the Analytics system of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Forecasting Principles is to:
- preserve explainable operational prediction continuity,
- maintain probabilistic operational awareness,
- support preparedness-oriented intelligence,
- and define how future operational possibilities become understandable throughout the ecosystem.

Forecasting represents:
- operational preparedness intelligence,
not merely:
- prediction engines,
- statistical projections,
- or automated planning systems.

Forecasting is one of the foundational operational intelligence systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats forecasting as:
- operational preparedness visibility,
not merely:
- future prediction.

Forecasting should help operators:
- anticipate operational continuity,
- understand possible workflow evolution,
- evaluate ecosystem stability,
- and improve operational readiness.

The system should preserve:
- explainable forecasting visibility,
- continuity-aware prediction context,
- probabilistic operational intelligence,
- and human-readable preparedness understanding.
