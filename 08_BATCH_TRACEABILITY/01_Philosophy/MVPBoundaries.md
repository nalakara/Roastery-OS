# MVP Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the MVP boundaries and implementation scope limitations for the Batch Traceability system inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of MVP Boundaries is to:
- preserve architectural focus,
- maintain operational realism,
- avoid premature enterprise complexity,
- and establish a scalable operational continuity foundation.

The MVP Batch Traceability system should prioritize:
- deterministic lineage continuity,
- operational genealogy visibility,
- transformation explainability,
- recall capability,
- and human-readable operational continuity.

The MVP is intended to establish:
- a strong operational traceability foundation,
not:
- industrial manufacturing governance infrastructure.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS evolves progressively.

The MVP Batch Traceability system should therefore focus on:
- practical operational continuity,
- deterministic genealogy,
- explainable transformation lineage,
- and operational recall visibility.

The MVP should avoid:
- enterprise manufacturing bureaucracy,
- industrial compliance infrastructure,
- and premature AI autonomy.

The goal is:
- operational usefulness first,
- ecosystem sophistication later.
