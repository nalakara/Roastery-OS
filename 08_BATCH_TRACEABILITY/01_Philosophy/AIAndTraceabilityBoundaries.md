# AI And Traceability Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophical and operational boundaries between AI systems and deterministic traceability systems inside Roastery OS.

The purpose of AI And Traceability Boundaries is to:
- preserve deterministic lineage integrity,
- maintain operational genealogy trust,
- support explainable operational continuity,
- and prevent autonomous mutation of critical traceability relationships.

AI systems may assist:
- operational analysis,
- anomaly detection,
- recall intelligence,
- and workflow optimization.

However:
AI must not replace deterministic operational traceability foundations.

This distinction is one of the core operational trust principles inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats AI as:
- operational intelligence assistance,
not:
- autonomous lineage authority.

AI should help operators:
- understand genealogy,
- analyze transformations,
- identify operational anomalies,
- and improve operational awareness.

However:
critical traceability continuity must remain:
- deterministic,
- explicit,
- traceable,
- auditable,
- and human-controlled.
