# Recall And Auditability

## Purpose

This document defines the operational recall and auditability philosophy used inside the Batch Traceability system of Roastery OS.

The purpose of Recall And Auditability is to:
- preserve operational explainability,
- maintain deterministic lineage verification,
- support downstream recall capability,
- and define how operational continuity remains auditable throughout the ecosystem.

Recall and auditability represent:
- operational trust continuity.

Recall capability and auditability are foundational trust systems inside Roastery OS.

---

# Core Philosophy

Roastery OS treats recall and auditability as:
- operational continuity verification,
not merely:
- compliance reporting,
- administrative inspection,
- or manufacturing bureaucracy.

Operational systems should preserve:
- explainable lineage,
- deterministic genealogy,
- traceable transformations,
- and verifiable operational history.

The system should support:
- operational trust,
- production explainability,
- and downstream accountability.
